## Follow-up corrections (apply to v6 image)

Keep everything else from v6 the same — four sector colors, sector text labels, faint dashed inter-Western boundaries, station assignments, geography, mood, palette.

**Only the red boundary line path needs adjustment.** The line must pass through or hug the following stations in the correct order, on the correct side. This reflects the actual 1961–1989 Berlin Wall trace.

### Stations the red line must touch or hug (north to south)

The red line should "graze" these stations so the boundary feels historically anchored:

|---|---|---|
| **Gesundbrunnen** | line passes on its **east** side | very close — the line should almost touch the station circle |
| **Wedding** | line passes on its **east** side | very close (Bernauer Straße is right there) |

| **Potsdamer Platz** | line **passes directly through or touches** the station ⚠️ | **most important fix** — Potsdamer Platz was bisected by the Wall in real life and became the "death strip" no-man's-land. ### Critical S-curve around Friedrichstrasse

Between **Hauptbahnhof** and **Brandenburger Tor**, the red line must make a small **S-curve eastward** so that Friedrichstrasse ends up on the EAST side (Soviet sector). Friedrichstrasse station was in East Berlin but served as the famous border-crossing point for Western travelers (Tränenpalast). After passing Friedrichstrasse on its west, the line curves back west to hug Brandenburger Tor's east side.

### Critical fix: Potsdamer Platz

The single most important correction. **The red line must pass directly through or immediately touch Potsdamer Platz.** Historically the entire plaza was a death strip from 1961 to 1989 — the Wall ran through it. In the current v6 image the line is clearly east of Potsdamer Platz with a visible gap; this is wrong. Redraw so the station label sits **on** the line or hugs its east side.
