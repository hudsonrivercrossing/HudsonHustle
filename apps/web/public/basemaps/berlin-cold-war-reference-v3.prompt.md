## Prompt

A vintage espionage-era map of divided Berlin, rendered as a declassified intelligence document on aged, slightly yellowed paper with worn, foxed edges. Hand-drawn ink cartography crossed with mid-century military briefing aesthetic. Top-down orthographic view. Aspect ratio 4:3.

### Two sector backgrounds, separated by a single line (no wall, no watchtowers, no fortifications)

- **West Berlin** — faded warm ivory with a faint dusty-rose undertone. Evokes Western newsprint, faded Kodachrome, 1960s NATO briefing maps. Slightly brighter, optimistic but worn.
- **East Berlin** — colder, smokier blue-grey with a faint olive-drab cast. Evokes Soviet topographic charts, cigarette smoke, concrete. Heavier, more shadowed, with a hint of cyanotype.
- The two sectors meet along a single thin oxidized iron-red dividing line — the sector boundary — winding north-to-south. Hand-inked, slightly uneven, like it was drawn on a typewriter-era situation map.

### Sector boundary path (historically accurate, 1961–1989 Berlin Wall trace)

- **North segment:** boundary runs along Bernauer Straße. **Wedding and Gesundbrunnen must be west of the line** (ivory / West Berlin). Do not let the line cross them or sit east of them.
- **Central segment:** passes east of Brandenburger Tor (gate sits on the East side, hugging the boundary), continues south past Potsdamer Platz on its east side.
- **Southern segment:** boundary must **bend east**, following the historical Treptow / Neukölln district edge, so that **Neukölln and Hermannplatz remain in West Berlin** (ivory).
- **Southwest:** continues west-southwest around Tempelhof and Schöneberg, closing toward Spandau direction.

### Station color assignments (each label sits on the correct sector color)

**West Berlin (ivory / warm rose):**
Spandau, Olympiastadion, Wannsee, Westkreuz, Schöneberg, Tempelhof, Südkreuz, Jungfernheide, Westhafen, Zoo, Potsdamer Platz, **Wedding**, **Gesundbrunnen**, **Neukölln**, **Hermannplatz**.

**East Berlin (blue-grey / olive-drab):**
Pankow, Schoenhauser (Prenzlauer Berg), Alexanderplatz, Friedrichstrasse, Brandenburger Tor (hugging east side of boundary), Frankfurter Allee, Warschauer, Ostkreuz.

**Boundary-adjacent:** Hauptbahnhof sits on or near the boundary; render it on the West side or directly on the line.

### Geography

- The Spree in tarnished steel-blue, meandering west-to-east with its characteristic loop around Spreebogen and Museum Island.
- Havel river and lakes in the far west, same tarnished blue.
- Tiergarten as a muted moss-green park in the western center.
- Tempelhofer Feld as a pale ochre oval in the south (echoing its airfield history).
- Grunewald forest as smoky stippled tree texture in the southwest.
- Volkspark Friedrichshain and Treptower Park as deeper olive shapes in the east.

### Street network

- Sepia-to-graphite ink street lines, slightly smudged, denser in the core, sparser in outer districts.
- Major boulevards thicker (Unter den Linden, Kurfürstendamm, Karl-Marx-Allee).
- A few faint registration crosshairs and corner ticks, as if printed from a classified plate.

### Mood and palette

- Overall: tobacco-stained paper, iron oxide, smoked glass, faded propaganda poster.
- Color story: bone-ivory, dusty rose, olive-drab, slate-blue, oxidized red, graphite.
- Subtle paper grain, foxing spots, faint coffee-ring stain in one corner.
- Evokes: John le Carré, *The Lives of Others*, Checkpoint Charlie postcards, declassified CIA Berlin Operations Base maps, 1961 situation reports.
- Optional micro-detail: small cross-tick markers at Bernauer Straße, Brandenburger Tor, and Checkpoint Charlie locations as historical landmark hints (no text labels for these).
- No legend, no compass rose required (a faint compass mark in one corner is acceptable).
- Station labels in restrained serif type, kept on the correct sector color background.
